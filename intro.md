# Gaza Evacuations

This interactive analysis visualizes the accuracy of evacuation orders issued by the Israeli Defense Forces (IDF) across Gaza.

## Displacement

- Cleaning and translating the evacuation order tweets we collected from the IDF's official Arabic channel.
- Filtering ACLED data to isolate relevant attacks by the IDF in Gaza post-7 October 2023.
- Converting the list of blocks mentioned in the evacuation orders to geographical polygons using official IDF block boundaries.
- Interactive maps displaying evacuation zones and nearby IDF attacks on the following days.

## Safezones

- Extract the location names of the designated safezones mentioned in the IDF's evacuation orders.
- Use ACLED data to determine instances of area's being under attack the same day(s) it was designated as a safezone for Gazans.
